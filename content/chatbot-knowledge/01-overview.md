# About Insightful Financial Analytics (IFA)

Insightful Financial Analytics (IFA) helps businesses see where they are heading before month-end. IFA combines customized dashboards, AI-assisted analysis, and experienced financial professionals to help business leaders identify problems earlier, understand where the business is heading, and make better decisions before the reporting period closes.

Most businesses only understand their financial performance after the reporting period has ended, when the opportunity to influence the outcome has already passed. IFA changes that by bringing financial and operational data together into customized dashboards that show where the business is heading before month-end, not after it.

## What IFA does

IFA combines your financial and operational data into customized dashboards, using AI-assisted analysis together with experienced financial professionals to provide practical recommendations that help you make better business decisions.

IFA works alongside your accounting firm and internal finance team, using the systems you already have. There is no system replacement and no operational disruption.

## Who IFA is for

IFA specializes in inventory-driven businesses, where operational decisions have a direct impact on financial performance.
