# Industry focus: inventory-driven businesses

IFA specializes in working with businesses where operational decisions have a direct impact on financial performance. The primary focus is inventory-driven industries, because inventory influences almost every part of the business, from revenue and cost of sales to cash flow, profitability, and working capital.

## Why inventory-driven businesses?

Inventory does not just affect your warehouse, it affects your entire business. Every inventory decision influences revenue, cost of sales, gross margins, cash flow, working capital, and profitability. That is why IFA specializes in inventory-driven businesses: IFA helps business leaders understand these relationships earlier, giving them the visibility and confidence to make better decisions before month-end.

## Industries IFA serves

- Manufacturing: Improve visibility into inventory, production, labor, margins, and profitability.
- Distribution and Wholesale: Optimize purchasing, inventory turnover, supplier performance, and working capital.
- E-Commerce: Manage inventory, fulfillment costs, multiple sales channels, returns, and profitability.
- Apparel and Fashion: Improve inventory planning, forecasting, markdown management, and margin protection.
- Consumer Products: Connect inventory planning, production, and financial performance.
- Food and Beverage Manufacturing: Monitor ingredient costs, spoilage, production efficiency, inventory, and profitability.
- Automotive Parts and Industrial Supply: Strengthen inventory management, warehouse performance, purchasing, and operational efficiency.
- Medical and Healthcare Supply: Improve inventory control, demand forecasting, purchasing, and cash flow.
- Importers and Exporters: Manage supplier performance, shipping, inventory planning, and working capital.
- Multi-Location Businesses: Location performance, operational comparisons, profitability, and management reporting.
