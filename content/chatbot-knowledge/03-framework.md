# The IFA Predictive Control Framework

The IFA Predictive Control Framework (IFA PCF) is IFA's proprietary methodology. It combines financial and non-financial data, customized dashboards, AI-assisted analysis, and continuous improvement into a structured system that helps businesses understand where they are heading before month-end.

## The Four Layers of the IFA PCF

1. Data Foundation: Build a strong financial foundation with clean, reliable, and standardized financial and operational data.
2. Operational Integration: Connect financial information with the operational drivers that influence business performance.
3. Predictive Intelligence: Transform financial and operational data into customized dashboards using AI-assisted analysis and experienced financial professionals to understand where the business is heading before month-end.
4. Continuous Improvement: Create a continuous feedback loop that turns insights into actions and continuously improves business performance over time.

## How IFA turns data into action (the 10 steps)

1. Establish a Strong Financial Foundation: Build on reliable financial information to support accurate analysis, forecasting, and better business decisions.
2. Define Revenue and Cost Drivers: Identify the operational activities that drive financial performance.
3. Define Data Sources: Identify the financial and operational data required to support decision-making.
4. Gather the Data: Collect data from existing systems, departments, and business units.
5. Clean and Integrate the Data: Standardize, validate, and integrate financial and operational data into a single reliable source.
6. Refine Financial Models and Forecasts: Build on existing budgets, forecasts, and financial models, or develop them where needed, to project business performance throughout the reporting period.
7. Generate Business Insights: Analyze each business unit to identify trends, risks, opportunities, and location-specific recommendations.
8. Develop Action Plans: Convert insights into practical action plans with clear ownership and measurable outcomes.
9. Build Customized Dashboards: Develop customized dashboards using existing financial and operational data to show where the business is heading, highlight emerging risks and opportunities, and recommend actions before month-end.
10. Review, Refine, and Improve: Meet regularly with leadership to review results, refine recommendations, and continuously improve business performance.
