# Contact IFA and the Free Financial Performance Assessment

## Get Clarity

You can start a conversation with IFA through the "Get Clarity" (Contact) page on the website. Share a few details (name, company name, and email) and IFA will be in touch with insights tailored to your business.

## Two simple questions

IFA starts with two simple questions:

1. When do you usually realize your business is off track?
2. When you find out, is there still enough time to do something about it?

Most businesses only discover financial problems after the reporting period has ended. IFA combines accounting, financial expertise, AI-assisted analysis, and customized dashboards to help you identify problems earlier, understand where your business is heading, and make better decisions before month-end.

## Free Financial Performance Assessment

IFA offers a Free Financial Performance Assessment. It helps you:

- Identify opportunities and emerging risks
- Understand where your business is heading before month-end
- Receive practical recommendations to improve business performance

To request the assessment, use the contact form on the "Get Clarity" page and provide your name, company name, and email.
